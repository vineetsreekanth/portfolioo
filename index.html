The Product

Health Tech · ABDM Sandbox–Approved
MedID

Portable, patient-owned health records for every Indian family

MedID is an ABDM-integrated health-records platform built as a reconciliation layer on top of ABHA — using Aadhaar-based deduplication to solve the duplicate-identity problem the document-based route leaves behind. It holds an ABDM sandbox approval (SBXID_035682) and is being built toward full ABDM compliance, with its first pilot running at a live pharmacy.

Aadhaar-based deduplication — one true identity per person
Family Health Tree with delegated Viewer / Manager access
AI report summaries from uploaded documents
Per-document privacy — restrict any record to one family member
Insurance extraction with family-aware name checks
~34,107 Karnataka facilities in a built-from-scratch database
